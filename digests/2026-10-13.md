# AI News Digest - 2026-10-13

## Top stories

### IMF names an AI-driven market correction as a top financial-stability risk
The IMF published its Global Financial Stability Report on Tuesday at the annual meetings in Bangkok. It named an "AI-driven market correction" as one of three main near-term risks, alongside a sharp rise in government bond yields and cyber threats. It warned that weaker or shorter-lived AI investment and profits could hit AI-linked stocks and credit, which now make up a large share of both markets. It also counted 14 hyperscaler-led "circular" deals since 2023, worth roughly $1.4tn in future revenue commitments, and noted that hyperscalers now issue about 10% of the world's investment-grade bonds, up from under 1% in 2024. **Why it matters:** This is the clearest official warning yet that the debt-funded AI buildout has become a risk to the whole financial system, not just to tech stocks.
Sources: [The Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/10/13/imf-warns-of-ai-driven-market-correction-in-global-economy/), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/imf-circular-ai-deals-financial-stability-report), [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/imf-warns-ai-market-correction-could-trigger-global-financial-turmoil/)

### Asian chip stocks fall after Broadcom CEO calls AI investing a "casino"
Speaking in Singapore on Oct 12, Broadcom CEO Hock Tan said many AI investors "have no clue what they are investing in." He also questioned the long-term economics of AI labs that are losing money. Asian chip stocks fell on Oct 13: the Kospi dropped more than 6% intraday, and SK Hynix and Samsung fell by roughly 9% at their lows. The slide was made worse by China's CXMT saying it will start mass production of next-generation DRAM this year, a market the two Korean firms dominate. **Why it matters:** One of the biggest AI-infrastructure suppliers is now openly questioning AI demand, on the same day the IMF issued its warning.
Sources: [CNBC TV18 via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cnbctv:23eb986ee094b:0/), [StartupFortune](https://startupfortune.com/chip-stocks-slide-across-asia-after-hock-tans-casino-remark-lands/), [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/10/13/samsung-sk-hynix-tumble-as-china-memory-threat-spooks)

### Meta releases Muse Wonder, its first frontier-class model
On Oct 12, Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full. Meta says the models score comparably to Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra, and it is promoting cross-conversation "active memory" as the key feature. Wonder costs $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens through the Meta Model API. Wonder Full is only available to Muse Code subscribers. Artificial Analysis ranks Full second on its index, behind Claude Opus 5.5. **Why it matters:** Meta is back among the frontier labs, and Zuckerberg separately said he is ready to spend $100–200bn on AI.
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/12/meta-releases-its-first-frontier-model-muse-wonder/), [Meta](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-wonder), [Cheeky Pint (Zuckerberg interview)](https://cheekypint.substack.com/p/muse-wonder-zuckerberg-and-wangs)

### Nvidia's share of China's AI-chip market falls to 34% as Huawei gains
IDC data shows Nvidia shipped 678,000 AI accelerators in China in the first half of 2026, down 44% from a year earlier, cutting its data-centre market share to 34%. Huawei shipped 572,000 units for a 28% share. Separately, the FT reports that Chinese companies bought more than 1 million Nvidia gaming and workstation chips this year to make up for a shortage of AI compute. **Why it matters:** US export controls are speeding up China's shift to domestic chips, while also pushing buyers toward workarounds.
Sources: [Business Recorder / Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40443949/nvidias-china-ai-chip-share-falls-as-huawei-closes-gap-report-finds), [The Standard (HK)](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/innovation/article/345296/Nvidias-China-AI-chip-share-falls-as-Huawei-closes-gap-report-finds), [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/9dbd99db-6b0c-43bd-b7be-849f129df098)

### UK keeps early access to test US frontier models
UK AI minister Kanishka Narayan confirmed on Monday that Britain's AI Security Institute will keep pre-release access to the newest US models. This reverses a reported White House request that OpenAI and Anthropic not share models with the UK before US review. The institute has had access to Google's Gemini 4 Argon, which has not been released publicly because of safety concerns. Narayan also warned that a breach of UK cyber defences seems "likely, if not inevitable." **Why it matters:** Allied testing of models before release was close to breaking down, and it has been restored for now.
Sources: [TechXplore / AFP](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-10-uk-retains-early-access-latest.html), [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/3335795/uk-retains-early-access-to-test-latest-us-ai-models)
