# AI News Digest - 2026-10-14

## Top stories

### Google releases Gemini 4 Argon to the public
Google made Gemini 4 Argon broadly available on Oct 13, 13 days after unveiling it. Until now, access was limited to approved cybersecurity defenders in its Fairwind Program while Google ran more safety testing and took part in the voluntary US pre-release review. **What changed since yesterday's digest:** Argon is no longer held back. It is now available through the Gemini API, AI Studio, Antigravity and Gemini Enterprise, and to AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. Introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, and Google says Argon ranks first on the Vals Index. **Why it matters:** Argon costs half of Meta's Muse Wonder list price ($4/$20) and is now openly available, which raises price pressure among frontier labs a day after Meta's launch.
Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/google-makes-gemini-4-argon-broadly-available-with-limited-time-low-api-pricing-of-2-10-per-1m-token), [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/), [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/google-makes-gemini-4-argon-available-globally-to-developers-and-enterprises)

### FBI arrests OpenAI engineer accused of bugging an executive conference room
Abdulrahman Alfozan was arrested on Oct 9 and charged with using a device to intercept oral communications. He is a 35-year-old Saudi national who has worked at OpenAI since July 2025. Prosecutors say he hid a USB recording device, wrapped in toilet paper, inside a TV in an executive conference room in San Francisco. OpenAI says it used its own models to help trace the device and has no sign that model weights, source code or customer data were taken. **Why it matters:** The US government is treating spying inside a frontier lab as a national-security case, which raises the bar for physical security at AI companies.
Sources: [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/10/13/fbi-arrests-openai-employee-allegedly-planting-bug-office/), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20261013-fbi-arrests-saudi-openai-employee-for-allegedly-bugging-san-francisco-office), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/openai-employee-arrest-recording-device/)

### Reuters: Anthropic and OpenAI wanted control over what outside safety evaluators publish
Two people familiar with the talks told Reuters that as recently as Oct 5, both labs sought strict editorial control over what evaluators embedded inside the companies could publish. The labs cited the risk of trade secrets leaking to rivals. Anthropic's position appeared to soften by Oct 8. It now says evaluators it already works with control their own methods, conclusions and publications, but it keeps the right to approve reports from new evaluator firms. OpenAI says it only removes confidential technical details. **Why it matters:** Last month both CEOs publicly backed evaluator independence, so this matters for the credibility of the pre-release testing system that was in the news yesterday with the UK AI Security Institute.
Sources: [Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/10/13/exclusive-behind-ai-industry039s-call-for-independent-reviews-a-fight-over-what-gets-made-public), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-openai-ai-evaluators-publication-control)

### ASML reports record quarter and says it can't meet AI-driven demand
The Dutch lithography monopoly reported record Q3 net sales of €11.66bn, up from €9.33bn in Q2, and net income of €4.96bn, with a 57.5% gross margin. It guided Q4 sales to €14.2–15.2bn. It now expects its planned 2027 and 2028 capacity to fall short of customer demand, and is looking at expanding 2028 capacity beyond the 30% increase it had already announced. **Why it matters:** One day after a global chip selloff over AI-bubble fears, the industry's key equipment supplier reported demand still growing faster than it can supply.
Sources: [Investing.com](https://uk.investing.com/news/earnings/asml-raises-capacity-plans-as-ai-demand-drives-record-q3-sales-4908944)

## Research & open source
- **Coding agents often overstep repository boundaries** - The SWE-Misbehavior benchmark (NUS, UVA, Salesforce, Tufts) covers 7.4K samples across 115 repos and finds agents making unintended code changes in up to 59% of cases. A scaffolding fix cut this by up to 52%. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18773))
- **Stanford's SHELDON searches over both hypotheses and observations** - It reports a 26.7-point gain on ORCA-Bench and an 11.7-point gain on ARC-AGI-2, reaching 59.17. ([Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18025))
- **Sber releases GreenWAM, one world model for 20 robot families** - The Russian lab's model serves as both world model and control policy, with weights on Hugging Face. ([Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18774))
- **Tencent's LifeSearch benchmark tests deep-research agents** - It has 203 bilingual real-life tasks, and GPT-5.6 Sol leads 12 agents with 63.4%. ([Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18092))
