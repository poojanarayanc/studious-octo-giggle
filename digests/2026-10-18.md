# AI News Digest - 2026-10-18

## Top stories

### Microsoft pulls out of Nscale's Essex data centre after grid warning
The Telegraph reported on Sunday that Microsoft has ended its agreement for Nscale's planned data centre in Loughton, Essex. Microsoft is one of Nscale's two biggest customers. Grid officials had told Nscale the site would not get power until the 2030s. Nscale told Reuters the grid problem affects all of London and doesn't touch its other contracts, and that it is looking at other power sources for the site. AI Weekly, citing the Telegraph, says the deal covered about 23,000 Nvidia chips and was the site's only announced customer. **Why it matters:** In the UK, grid connections rather than chips are now what's stopping AI projects, and this hit comes just after Nscale filed for a US IPO.
Sources: [Reuters via KFGO](https://kfgo.com/2026/10/18/microsoft-pulls-out-of-uk-data-center-deal-with-nscale-telegraph-reports/), [The Telegraph](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/business/2026/10/18/microsoft-axes-ai-deal-setback-uk-data-centre-pioneer/)

### Chip-stock sell-off ends the week as the worst losing streak since 2008
*Update to yesterday's item on the sell-off.* Final figures show the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 16% over eight sessions, its longest losing run since October 2008. On the last day it fell 3.83% to 11,137.29, and all 30 stocks in the index closed lower. Memory makers fell hardest: SK Hynix lost about 6.6% and Micron about 5.6%. CoreWeave fell 11.9% as investors questioned its debt load. S3 Partners data put short sellers' paper gains at about $32B. **Why it matters:** Investors are now pricing in the risk that AI infrastructure built on debt won't pay off on schedule.
Sources: [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-chip-stocks-longest-losing-streak-2008/), [Quartz](https://qz.com/chip-short-sellers-32-billion-paper-gains-selloff-101626), [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/4653f52e-d964-4a23-90e0-9b2091a94646)

### Anthropic signs a long-term lease for its first Canadian data centre, in Nova Scotia
EverWind Fuels announced a data-centre campus at Point Tupper, Nova Scotia, that it says could cost C$10B. Anthropic later confirmed it is the tenant for the first phase, 95 MW due in late 2027. A further 325 MW in 2028 would take the campus to 420 MW, but that still depends on grid studies and approvals. Anthropic usually buys compute through cloud providers. Here it is leasing directly from the site owner. Nova Scotia's NDP leader asked how the government would hold the company to its promises to the local community. **Why it matters:** AI labs are starting to lock in power and sites themselves instead of going through the big cloud providers.
Sources: [The Logic](https://thelogic.co/news/anthropic-data-centre-cape-breton-nova-scotia-everwind/), [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-anthropic-signs-onto-10-billion-nova-scotia-data-centre-project/), [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/33910)

### Anthropic's Jack Clark tells IMF meeting AI could push US GDP to $44T by 2030
Clark spoke on Saturday at the IMF and World Bank annual meetings in Bangkok. He cited an internal Anthropic scenario in which US output reaches about $44 trillion by 2030, up from just over $30 trillion now. The scenario assumes AI keeps improving. He said countries need to work together on "unusual and serious rules" and that time to act is running out. **Why it matters:** At these meetings AI was discussed both as a source of growth and as a risk to financial stability if valuations collapse, and the chip sell-off had just shown that risk.
Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/17/ai-us-gdp-anthropics-clark.html), [US News/Reuters](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-10-17/anthropics-clark-says-ais-impact-accelerating-quickly-and-co)

### Sam Altman confirms OpenAI is building a humanoid robot
Asked on the Sources podcast whether OpenAI would build a humanoid robot, Altman said "We will." He told Fortune he expects a demo in 2027 but thinks it will be a few more years before robots are walking the streets. He said building the AI "brain" is harder than building the body. **Why it matters:** OpenAI is now openly competing with Tesla and XPeng on humanoid robots, adding to its work on models and consumer devices.
Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2285040/openai-humanoid-robot-sam-altman-statement-real-challenge/)

## Research & open source
- **Frontier models find counterexamples to a 1967 fusion-physics conjecture** - In the TheoPhysEval study, GPT-6 Astra Pro, Gemini 4 and Claude models produced hand-checked counterexamples to Harold Grad's conjecture about plasma equilibria in stellarator fusion reactors. All 50 problems together cost $116 in API fees. ([Maccus](https://maccus.fr/en/ai-physics-fusion-conjecture/))
- **Anthropic publishes data on how AI is changing its own hiring** - Junior hires in technical teams fell from 27% to 9%. Hours of AI-agent work overtook human hours in March and reached a 6:1 ratio by August. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/employment-at-anthropic))
- **OpenCode says a "distillation attack" exposed 1.4% of its Console users** - The AI coding-agent company says stolen API keys are being used to run up charges. It is working with Google, OpenAI and Anthropic and has told customers to revoke their keys. ([Hugging News](https://huggingnews.com/cybersecurity/opencode-confirms-distillation-attack-hit-14percent-of-console-users-51c80aca))
