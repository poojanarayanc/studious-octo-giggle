# AI News Digest - 2026-10-19

## Top stories

### Investors push back on Nscale's $30B IPO target, days after Microsoft quit its Essex site
*Update to yesterday's Nscale item.* Bloomberg reports that a group of investors wants London-based Nscale to list at a valuation of $16–19B, not the roughly $30B it is seeking. They point to Microsoft leaving its planned Essex site and to the collapse of the IPO by Firmus, an Australian AI data-centre builder. Formal marketing is on hold while Nscale answers questions from the SEC. Its filing shows $140.6M in revenue and a $1.02B net loss for the first half of the year, plus "material weaknesses" in its financial controls. **Why it matters:** This is the first clear sign that last week's sell-off in AI infrastructure stocks is lowering private valuations as well.
Sources: [Quartz (citing Bloomberg)](https://qz.com/nscale-ipo-valuation-investor-pushback-101926), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nscale-ipo-valuation-investor-pushback)

### Australia's biggest data-centre operator halts trading over a power delay at its Melbourne AI campus
NextDC asked the ASX to halt trading in its shares on Monday so it can assess how a delay in power supply to its M3 Melbourne site will affect its FY27 guidance. Its board has not yet met to consider the delay. The company expects to make an announcement within two days, and trading will resume on Oct 21 if it hasn't. The site has also faced local complaints because it sits close to homes. **Why it matters:** One day after Nscale's UK grid problem, a second major operator on another continent is being slowed by power supply, not by chips.
Sources: [Reuters via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L4N465017:0-australia-s-nextdc-in-trading-halt-ahead-of-update-on-melbourne-data-centre-power-delay/), [Capital Brief](https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/nextdc-enters-trading-halt-as-melbourne-m3-data-centre-faces-power-delays-50b36934-e80a-4ba9-bc33-1c731e3ebc85/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-18/nextdc-flags-earnings-risk-on-melbourne-data-center-power-d)

### Anthropic commits 5% of its shares to a new Public Benefit Fund
Anthropic said on Oct 18 that it will put 5% of its shares into a new wholly owned company, if its shareholders approve. The board can add another 2% for every 50% rise in the company's value above a set baseline. The fund could make large donations to nonprofits or pay money directly to particular groups, such as workers hurt by AI. None of the shares can be used for lobbying or political donations. **Why it matters:** This is a new kind of commitment from a frontier lab: it ties how much it gives away to its own valuation, starting with money for workers displaced by AI.
Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/public-benefit-fund)

### Meta says a Claude-linked tool caused the wrongful Instagram suspension of anti-Trump group No Kings
On Sunday, Meta's communications chief Andy Stone said a tool linked to Anthropic's Claude had read the No Kings Coalition's profiles, posts and hashtags in short bursts at about four times human speed. That set off Meta's bot detection. The account was down for about 90 minutes on Saturday, during early-voting events ahead of the Nov 3 midterms. Stone apologised but didn't say which tool it was or who was running it. **Why it matters:** Platform defences built to catch bots are now catching AI agents, and in this case it happened to a political group in the middle of an election.
Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-claude-no-kings-instagram-suspension)

### Tenstorrent raises $1.5B at a $6.2B valuation
Jim Keller's AI chip company closed a $1.5B Series E led by Trace Capital and Innovation X. Fidelity, Morgan Stanley, ARK Invest and T. Rowe Price also took part. The valuation is more than double the $2.6B it reached in late 2024. The money will go toward mass production of its RISC-V-based Galaxy servers. **Why it matters:** Investors are still putting large sums into alternatives to Nvidia even after chip stocks' worst run since 2008.
Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/19/chip-legend-jim-kellers-tenstorrent-raises-1-5b-to-expand-its-open-compute-ecosystem/)

## One to watch
**Websites and banks are starting to push back on AI agents.** Meta's bot defences shut down a political group's account because of a Claude-linked tool. US banks including Schwab and JPMorgan now say they block outside agents from client accounts. At the same time, Stripe told Upstarts that purchases made by AI agents through its Link wallet rose 38-fold in September. The open question is whether agents get proper access, through official APIs and liability rules, or keep running into bot filters. Coming up this week: Nvidia's GTC Berlin, where the German deal may be announced, and NextDC's update on its power delay, due by Oct 21.
