# AI News Digest - 2026-10-21

## Top stories

### Anthropic's draft IPO filing shows $16.3B in first-half revenue and up to $750B in compute commitments
*Update to yesterday's item on the OpenAI and Anthropic fundraising race. This is the first look at Anthropic's draft S-1.* The Wall Street Journal has reviewed the draft. It shows $16.3B of revenue in the first half of 2026, up from $1.1B a year earlier, and a net loss of $80.7B. About $72B of that loss is non-cash accounting charges on financial instruments Anthropic issued to raise money. Compute spending was nearly $9B in the half, more than the $7.3B spent in all of 2025. The draft says Anthropic could commit up to $750B to compute in the coming years, including deals it cannot cancel with Google ($111B), AWS ($110B) and Microsoft ($31B). It also warns of three risks:
- Those cloud providers increasingly treat Anthropic as a competitor, which makes compute harder to get.
- About 20% of revenue comes from just two customers.
- A catastrophic AI incident could cost more than its insurance covers.

The public filing is expected within weeks. **Why it matters:** This is the first detailed look at a frontier lab's finances that investors will be asked to buy into, and it shows that compute commitments, not revenue, are the main variable.
Sources: [Dealroom (summarising WSJ)](https://dealroom.co/news/164610-anthropics-draft-s-1-reveals-16-3b-revenue-80-7b-loss-and-up-to-750b-com/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/video/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-reveals-807b-net-loss-210816826.html)

### ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent order more than $35B of export-compliant Nvidia chips
Reuters reports the three companies have ordered more than 3 million RTX PRO 5500 cards at about $11,400 each. The RTX PRO 5500 is a workstation chip that complies with US export rules, and they mainly want it for running models (inference). Total orders from China are above 6 million cards. Nvidia has told partners it may make no more than 3 million worldwide in 2027, and some buyers may get only 5–10% of what they ordered. **Why it matters:** US export rules have not stopped Chinese demand for Nvidia chips. They have pushed it toward weaker chips, and supply, not demand, now limits how much China gets.
Sources: [GV Wire (Reuters)](https://gvwire.com/2026/10/20/bytedance-alibaba-tencent-place-over-35-billion-worth-of-nvidia-chip-orders/), [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114183/bytedance-alibaba-and-tencent-order-nvidia-rtx-pro-5500-chips-worth-over-dollars35-billion/index.html)

### NextDC loses about 42% when it resumes trading after cutting guidance
*Update to yesterday's "One to watch".* NextDC, an Australian data-centre operator, closed down 41.7% at A$5.35 when it returned to the ASX. It has cut its FY27 revenue and earnings guidance because power upgrades for its M3 data centre in Melbourne's West Footscray were suspended after opposition from local residents and others. The electricity distributor, Jemena, doesn't expect to restart the work before February 2027. The shares are down about 65% over the past year. **Why it matters:** This is a clear case of AI data-centre growth being held back by local opposition and power supply, not demand.
Sources: [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-21/asx-markets-business-live-news-oct-21-2026/107290728), [IBTimes Australia](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/nextdc-shares-plunge-melbourne-data-centre-power-delay-1877083)

### Huang's GTC Berlin keynote: factory-built AI data centres and a safety design for agents
*Follow-up to yesterday's "One to watch".* Nvidia announced DSX POD, a reference design for complete Vera Rubin AI data centres that partners build in modular containers and test before delivery. Nvidia says on-site setup can fall from six months to 14 days, once the site's power and other utilities are ready. It also launched the OpenShell Reference Architecture, an open-source runtime that sandboxes agents and controls what they are allowed to do. Huang said Vera Rubin is in full production. No new figures on the German government deal came out. **Why it matters:** Nvidia is pitching Europe a complete package of hardware, models and agent controls, while the real limit is how fast sites can get power.
Sources: [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/gtc-berlin-2026-keynote/), [Futu News](https://news.futunn.com/en/post/1001792335/jensen-huang-s-gtc-berlin-keynote-ai-factory-deployment-can)

## One to watch
**Power is becoming the main limit on AI growth.** This week NextDC lost about 40% of its value over a delayed power upgrade. Nvidia's 14-day DSX POD timeline only starts "once site utilities are ready". Anthropic's draft filing warns that getting enough compute could hold back its growth. The next tests are Anthropic's public S-1, expected within weeks, and the hyperscalers' upcoming earnings. Both will show whether investors keep funding huge compute commitments when getting power to sites is the slowest step.
