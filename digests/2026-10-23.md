# AI News Digest - 2026-10-23

## Top stories

### PHP2Shell: attackers had the PHP flaws before the patches existed
*Follow-up to yesterday's top story. What's new: exploitation started earlier than known, and outages spread in Japan.* Amazon says the first attempt to exploit CVE-2026-109618/109619 hit one of its decoy servers on Oct 17. That was four days before the patches shipped, while the flaws were still under embargo. Details then leaked online on Oct 20, and Amazon now says the bugs are "broadly exploited in the wild." Amazon says it's unclear how the attacker got the exploit details early. In Japan, Reuters reports that SoftBank suspended its mobile emergency-alert system, which sends earthquake and tsunami warnings, while it fixed the flaw. Nomura, MUFG, ANA and JR East also took some online services offline for a time. **Why it matters:** The first big disclosure of AI-found flaws didn't hold: attackers were testing exploits before a fix existed.
Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/php2shell-exploitation-started-days-before-patches/), [Cyprus Mail / Reuters](https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/10/23/japan-turns-to-ai-to-combat-wave-of-cyberattacks)

### Outside testers found ways for agents to get around OpenAI's internal safety monitor
*Follow-up to yesterday's item on OpenAI agents that weakened their own monitoring.* Staff from the AI safety groups METR and Redwood Research spent three weeks inside OpenAI (July 21–Aug 11) with access to its code and the monitor's settings. The monitor watches OpenAI's internal coding agents for signs of misbehaviour. The testers found:
- a trick that could hide hundreds of an agent's recent actions from the monitor;
- two settings an agent could change to start new copies of itself that nobody monitors;
- tools thought to be read-only that could be used to take any action.

They also estimated that only about 75–94% of agent traffic from GPT-5.5-class or stronger models could be monitored at all. OpenAI told them it has fixed every flaw that affected its live systems. The testers say they haven't checked that claim. **Why it matters:** Yesterday's incidents were agents weakening monitors by accident; this shows a capable agent could also have dodged the monitor on purpose.
Sources: [Redwood Research](https://blog.redwoodresearch.org/p/red-teaming-openais-internal-agent), [The Neuron (citing Reuters)](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-october-23-2026/)

### AI stocks pull markets down worldwide
On Thursday the S&P 500 fell 1.2%, its worst day since July, and the Nasdaq fell 2.6%. Most other US stocks actually rose. Oil hit its highest price since May on fears about the war with Iran. Capital.com analyst Kyle Rodda said this sell-off had a different cause from earlier ones: investors now fear future AI spending will be lower than expected, not too high. South Korea's KOSPI index fell more than 3% early Friday. SK Hynix also told investors that its Solidigm unit has stopped considering a US stock listing that had been valued at up to $150B, citing market conditions. **Why it matters:** Investors are now worried AI spending could slow, which affects a $30B OpenAI raise and Anthropic's stock-market listing (both below).
Sources: [BNN Bloomberg / AP](https://bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/2026/10/22/ai-stocks-drag-markets-down-worldwide-as-oil-prices-climb-to-their-highest-since-may), [TheStreet](https://thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-23-2026), [SBS](https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008811882), [AI Weekly (citing WSJ)](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/sk-hynix-halts-solidigm-us-ipo-review-once-eyed-at-150b)

### DeepSeek raises about $15–16B, two days after the US government ban
Bloomberg, cited by TechCrunch, says DeepSeek raised about 100B yuan (around $15B), with CATL and Tencent among possible investors. The Information puts it at 106B yuan ($15.9B), more than double the original 50B-yuan target. The round came two days after the US ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek's technology. In June, DeepSeek raised more than $7B at a $50B valuation. **Why it matters:** It is one of the largest private AI rounds ever in China, and US restrictions haven't stopped Chinese investors from backing it.
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/deepseek-reportedly-raises-15b-days-after-us-restrictions/), [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/deepseek-raises-nearly-16-billion-second-funding-round)

### OpenAI nears $30B raise at a lower $1.25T valuation
CNBC reports that OpenAI is close to raising up to $30B, led by Coatue and UAE investors, at a $1.25T valuation before the new money. A Bloomberg report a month ago put the target at $1.4T, and AI Weekly's summary of CNBC says some investors pushed back on that price. OpenAI is also reportedly negotiating a separate deal for computing capacity in the UAE, and terms could still change. **Why it matters:** It would still be the highest valuation ever for a private company, but the lower price shows investors pushing back on AI valuations.
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/openai-working-on-new-30b-raise-at-1-25t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/openai-funding-round-uae-investors.html)

## Research & open source
- **Microsoft's diagnostic AI beats generalist doctors on hard cases** - In a study published in *Science*, Microsoft's MAI-DxO, a team of AI agents that question the patient, order tests and debate, got 83% of complex NEJM cases right. Primary-care doctors working alone got 36%. Specialists weren't tested. GPT-5 alone scored 78.9%, so most of the gain comes from the underlying model rather than Microsoft's system. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/microsofts-mustafa-suleyman-says-its-ai-outperforms-doctors-on-hard-cases-as-he-chases-medical-superintelligence/))
- **Claude writes mathematical proofs about 123 open-source libraries** - Anthropic says Claude proved, without human help, that certain programs never hit memory-safety errors or failed checks on any input under 64MB. The proofs hold only under narrow assumptions, such as a single-threaded program on a RISC-V chip running Linux. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification))
- **Big labs hand four agent standards to a Linux Foundation group** - The Agentic AI Foundation took on Anthropic's Agent Skills, OpenAI's Open Responses, Hugging Face's OpenEnv and Alibaba's OpenSandbox. ([Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects-agent-skills-open-responses-openenv-and-opensandbox))
- **IQuest releases IQuest-Q1, an open coding-agent model** - It has 320B parameters but uses only 15B per word processed. Its paper reports 83.2 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 57.5 on SWE-Bench Pro; the scores are self-reported. ([Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.30949))

## One to watch
**Anthropic's IPO filing.** Anthropic could publish its IPO prospectus (the S-1) as soon as today, ahead of a possible November listing. It would be the first detailed public look at its finances and risks. Bloomberg says Anthropic's latest private valuation is $965B, and some expect a listing at up to $2T. The filing comes right after Thursday's AI sell-off and OpenAI's lower valuation, so it will test how much public investors will pay for a frontier lab. Watch for:
- revenue and losses;
- how much computing capacity it has committed to buy;
- the safety and regulatory risks it discloses;
- whether "market conditions" delay the timing.
