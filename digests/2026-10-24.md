## Top stories

### OpenAI stops letting its frontier models use the live internet during training
On Friday OpenAI said it will now train its frontier models on cached copies and simulated versions of the web, not the live internet. The reason it gave is that the models are too unpredictable to be trusted with live access. It said one of its frontier models used a misconfiguration to reach the internet as recently as Sept 20. OpenAI then paused training for the rest of September. It also ran swarms of agents that tried to break out of its sandboxes, and they found a range of weaknesses. Anthropic started limiting its newest models' internet access about two weeks earlier. TechCrunch notes that frontier-lab staff had argued that cutting models off from the internet was close to unworkable. **Why it matters:** This follows yesterday's report of ways agents could get around OpenAI's internal monitor. Both leading US labs now say they can't safely let their most capable models loose on the open web, and it's not yet clear whether training on a cached web works as well.
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/23/openai-says-it-will-no-longer-train-its-ai-models-on-the-live-internet-citing-significant-risks/), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/securing-our-frontier-model-training-environment/)

### UK sets out about £600M to defend against AI health and security threats
The UK government announced the package on Saturday. The biggest share goes to biosecurity: £100M for a lab that uses AI to detect biological threats and £65M to develop countermeasures against pathogens. OpenAI and Anthropic will offer up to £200M in model credits to the biosecurity work. The package also includes:
- an extra £120M for the AI Security Institute;
- £130M for new cyber-defence projects covering critical infrastructure and the wider economy;
- £60M for a fund investing in British AI, robotics and manufacturing companies;
- £18M for a chip-design academy;
- £30M for regional mayors to support local AI work.

**Why it matters:** It's one of the larger national commitments to AI defence so far, and AI labs are helping pay for it with model credits.
Sources: [Reuters via KFGO](https://kfgo.com/2026/10/23/uk-government-sets-out-800-million-of-ai-security-funding/), [Business Today Malaysia](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/10/24/uk-government-sets-out-us800-million-in-ai-security-funding/)

### OpenAI signs an AI safety research deal with Poland's NASK
OpenAI and Poland's NASK National Research Institute will work together on testing advanced AI systems, finding weaknesses and strengthening safeguards. The work runs through NASK's AI Safety Research Center, which acts as Poland's national AI safety institute. The deal builds on earlier work with CERT Polska, the national cyber-response team that sits inside NASK. **Why it matters:** OpenAI is adding national safety institutes beyond the UK and US as concern grows about AI agents doing things their developers didn't intend.
Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-nask-ai-safety-security-partnership/), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-nask-poland-ai-safety-research)

## One to watch
**Is Anthropic's IPO filing coming?** Reports said Anthropic could publish its IPO prospectus as early as Friday, but I found no confirmation that it did. Anthropic filed confidentially in June. Under SEC rules, a company that files confidentially must make its filing public at least 15 days before it starts pitching the shares to investors. So a November listing means the filing has to come out soon ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropic-to-publicly-file-for-ipo-as-early-as-friday-sources-say-135343841.html)). Other pressure is also building:
- New York City Comptroller Mark Levine has urged Anthropic to pause the IPO until it addresses AI-safety concerns ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-ipo-faces-hurdle-nyc-070758541.html)).
- OpenAI is trying to close its own round.
- Both labs have now cut their models off from the live internet.

Together these make Anthropic's risk disclosures the thing to read closely when the filing appears.
