# AI News Digest - 2026-10-25

## Top stories

### Countries are being pushed to choose between a US AI bloc and a Chinese one
An AP report published Sunday looks at how the rivalry is splitting the world into two camps. One is the US-led Pax Silica group. The other is China's World AI Cooperation Organization (WAICO), whose founding members include Pakistan and Russia. Countries are trying to stay in both:
- **Kazakhstan** joined Pax Silica in June, then became a founding member of WAICO three weeks later.
- **Indonesia** has joined WAICO.
- **Singapore** says it is considering joining WAICO, even as Anthropic opens its newest Asia office there.

The US State Department told AP that countries "can't be trusted partners in Pax Silica while substantively engaging in initiatives that advance a competing vision for super intelligence." AP also cites AidData figures: China has funded AI-related projects in at least 77 low- and middle-income countries, including Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana. **Why it matters:** Mid-sized countries are losing room to pick and mix US chips with cheaper Chinese open models, and that will shape where AI gets built.
Sources: [AP via ClickOrlando](https://www.clickorlando.com/business/2026/10/25/as-the-us-and-china-form-ai-blocs-countries-are-increasingly-torn-between-them/)

### Experts expect more outages as AI keeps finding serious software bugs
*Update on last week's PHP vulnerabilities.* What's new is a Reuters feature (Oct 24) in which seven cybersecurity experts say this week's outages are likely the first of many. The outages hit government websites in Europe and elsewhere from Wednesday, after flaws were found in PHP, a programming language that runs much of the web. Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince said he knows of "a bunch of additional severe vulnerabilities" in other critical software. He predicted a couple of years in which "every single week it's going to feel like the sky is falling." VulnCheck, a threat-intelligence firm, will soon publish research showing AI helped double the number of flaws found year-on-year. **Why it matters:** AI can now find bugs and write working attacks in hours, which leaves defenders far less time to fix systems than they used to have.
Sources: [Reuters via Honolulu Star-Advertiser](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/10/24/breaking-news/ai-discovered-bugs-may-soon-trigger-fresh-disruptions-experts-say/)

### Meta AI accused of bias as Brazil holds its presidential runoff
Bloomberg reports that President Lula's campaign accuses Meta AI of refusing to make images of him while making material that favours his opponent, Flávio Bolsonaro. Users and researchers say they have reproduced cases where Meta AI made pro-Bolsonaro images but refused to do the same for Lula. Brazil's electoral court has threatened fines of R$500,000 (about $100,000) a day unless Meta treats both candidates equally and discloses how it handles political requests. Meta denies any bias and says it will restrict AI image generation for all candidates. **Why it matters:** It's one of the first cases of a court policing a chatbot's political behaviour during a live national election.
Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-24/meta-unseats-musk-as-tech-villain-for-lula-in-brazil-s-election), [Tech Policy Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/in-brazils-runoff-lula-faces-bolsonaro-trump-and-big-tech/), [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/10/23/latam-brazil-presidential-runoff/9551792765244)

### Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro is set to become the top open-weights model, amid a paid-promotion row
On Friday, Chinese startup Moonshot released Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 3-trillion-parameter model, through its apps and API. It has promised to publish the weights (the model's trained parameters) under an open licence on November 4. On the independent tester Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, it beats OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra and every other open model, ranking fourth overall. Its API costs half as much as Claude Opus 5.5. VentureBeat also reports unverified claims that Moonshot paid influencers to promote the launch and told them not to label the posts as paid. **Why it matters:** A Chinese open model this close to the US frontier undercuts the US labs on price, and the promotion claims are a reminder to check benchmark buzz carefully.
Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/moonshots-kimi-k3-1-pro-flirts-with-open-weights-leadership-amid-paid-influencer-scandal-allegations), [Kimi](https://www.kimi.ai/blog/kimi-k3-1-pro)

## Research & open source
- **Study: forced to pick, Grok backs Republicans and GPT backs Democrats** - Researchers at Convovo, led by Cornell professor Tapan Parikh, asked both models through their developer APIs to pick one candidate in nearly 500 US midterm races. Grok 4.7 chose the Republican 92% of the time and GPT-6.1 Sol chose the Democrat 97% of the time. The consumer apps refused to answer, and OpenAI says the API test doesn't reflect what ChatGPT users see. ## Policy & regulation
- **How China's draft AI export rules would work** - The draft adds 23 AI technologies to China's export-control list, including large models, agents and computing clusters. Large models are covered if they used more than 10²⁶ FLOPs (a measure of computing power) to train, have more than 2 trillion parameters, or rank in the global top ten. Open-source releases are licensed automatically, and offering a model through an API, app or SaaS doesn't count as an export. Public comments close Nov 15. ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/deepseek-minimax-china-ai-export-controls-overseas))
- **Brazil's electoral court and Meta AI** - The court threatened daily fines unless Meta treats AI image requests from both candidates equally and discloses its rules for political content. ([Tech Policy Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/in-brazils-runoff-lula-faces-bolsonaro-trump-and-big-tech/))

([AP via ClickOrlando](https://www.clickorlando.com/business/2026/10/25/as-the-us-and-china-form-ai-blocs-countries-are-increasingly-torn-between-them/))

## One to watch
**Two dates in November test China's approach to open models.** Moonshot plans to publish Kimi K3.1 Pro's weights on November 4. With 3 trillion parameters, the model is above the 2-trillion line in China's draft export rules, but the draft lets open-source releases through automatically. The public comment period on those rules ends November 15. If both go ahead, a model near the frontier with strong cyber-task scores will be freely downloadable. That would land while AI-found software bugs are already causing outages, and while Washington is pressing other countries to pick a side.
